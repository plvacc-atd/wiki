SIDs and STARs are published routes that standardize how aircraft leave and arrive at busy airports.


A **SID** (Standard Instrument Departure) guides an aircraft from the runway to the en-route airway network. A **STAR** (Standard Terminal Arrival Route) does the reverse, leading it from the airway network toward the approach.

The benefits are practical:

- Less radio traffic, since the route is already known
- Predictable flows that keep departures and arrivals apart
- Built-in noise abatement and terrain clearance

Instead of dictating every turn, a controller can say "cleared via the SID" or "descend via the STAR", and both sides know exactly what to expect.
